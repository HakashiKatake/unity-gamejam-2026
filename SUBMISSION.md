# itch.io page draft

**Title:** House of 77

**Short description:** Survive a cursed casino. Spend your chips on power or luck. Break the house.

## Description

The house has a number with your name on it.

Fight enchanted warriors with a storm of magical blades. Collect chips, buy permanent upgrades, or spend your winnings to rig the next lucky-number spin. Match the number and unleash Jackpot Overdrive: double damage and seven-blade bursts.

Four waves. Four spins. One possessed slot machine standing between you and freedom.

Even bad luck has a limit: miss twice and the house owes you a jackpot.

Read the enemy windups, dodge their strikes, and earn a seven-blade burst every seven kills.

**Controls:** WASD / arrows to move. Mouse to aim. Hold left-click to shoot. Space to dash. Esc to pause. Keyboard and mouse required; desktop browser recommended.

**Run length:** approximately 6–8 minutes, excluding time spent making upgrade choices.

**Team:** Saurabh and Prince.

**Credits:** paste the asset attribution and source links from `CREDITS.md`.

**AI assistance:** AI tools assisted with design, C# programming, editor automation, shaders, UI, tests, and build preparation. All character, machine, icon, and background artwork was supplied through pre-existing imported packs. No generative artwork or audio was created for the submission.
